📖 How to Use

Step 1: Click "Copy to Clipboard" button above

Step 2: Paste into your email client:

  • Gmail: Settings → See all settings → Signature → Paste (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V)
  • Outlook: Settings → Mail → Compose and reply → Paste
  • Apple Mail: Mail → Settings → Signatures → Paste

💡 Tip: If auto-copy doesn't work, manually select the signature in the preview box above, press Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on Mac), then paste into your email client.
