Quick Spam Prevention Tips

🔐 Email Authentication

Setting up proper email authentication is crucial for deliverability:

SPF (Sender Policy Framework): Verifies that emails come from authorized servers for your domain.

DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail): Adds a digital signature to verify email authenticity.

DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication): Tells receiving servers how to handle emails that fail SPF/DKIM checks.

✍️ Content Best Practices

  • Avoid ALL CAPS in subject lines
  • Limit exclamation marks (!!!) and urgency words
  • Include more text than images
  • Use reputable links (avoid URL shorteners)
  • Keep HTML clean and simple
  • Include an unsubscribe link for marketing emails

📊 Sender Reputation

  • Maintain consistent sending patterns
  • Monitor bounce rates (keep below 5%)
  • Process unsubscribes promptly
  • Use a dedicated IP for high-volume sending
  • Warm up new domains gradually
